ABOUT US

Welcome To Indigo Bookshop!

Indigo closed three high-profile stores in Toronto in the spring of 2014, including the World's Biggest Bookstore, which it acquired when it bought Chapters. In June 2014, Reisman said the company was headed into a new phase, selling a much higher percentage of non-book items. In late 2017, it was announced that it would expand to the United States, with its first location opening in The Mall at Short Hills in October 2018. In September 2022, Reisman stepped down as CEO and became executive chair, with Peter Ruis being named CEO.In June 2023, Reisman announced her retirement, effective August 22nd 2023. This came after four out of ten board members stepped down, citing loss of confidence and mistreatment from the board leadership. In September 2023, founder Heather Reisman returned as chief executive officer, after Peter Ruis abruptly resigned earlier in the month.
